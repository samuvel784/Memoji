# Privacy Policy – Emogy: The Memory Game

_Last updated: 26 October 2026_

_Applies to the Emogy Android app (package `com.vurka.emogy`)._

Emogy is an offline memory game. This policy explains what the app does with your information.
In short: **the app itself collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.**

## Summary

* No account, no sign-in and no profile.
* No advertising, analytics, crash reporting or social SDKs.
* No internet permission, so the app cannot transmit data even in principle.
* All game data stays in the app's private storage on your device.
* The only permission is `VIBRATE`, used for haptic feedback.

## Information we collect

**None.** Emogy has no servers, no account system and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you do in the
app is sent off your device: there is no advertising ID, no analytics and no tracker of any kind.
The game data that stays on your phone is described in the next section.

## Information stored on your device

To make the game work, Emogy saves the following in its private app storage:

* **Progress:** completed levels, stars, best times and best move counts, and your coins.
* **Emoji album:** the emoji you have unlocked by matching pairs.
* **Daily Challenge:** the stored board day, best time and moves, completion count and daily streak.
* **Settings:** sound on/off, volume levels, haptic feedback, theme mode, selected card-back theme,
  Zen mode and Assist mode.
* **First-run flag:** whether the Welcome sheet has already been shown.
* **Unfinished run:** a snapshot of a level in progress so it can be restored after the app closes.
  It expires after 24 hours.
* **Statistics:** games played, perfect games, card flips and other counters shown on the Statistics
  screen.

This data is used only to run the game and restore your progress. It is stored inside the app's
private sandbox, which other apps cannot read. We cannot see it and we do not receive a copy.

You can erase it at any time:

* **Settings → Reset All Game Progress** clears progress, coins, album, daily results and statistics.
  It keeps your sound and volume, haptics, light/dark theme mode, Zen and Assist settings. The
  selected card back returns to the free default because purchased card backs are cleared with the
  rest of the progress.
* **Android Settings → Apps → Emogy → Storage → Clear storage** removes everything.
* **Uninstalling the app** removes everything stored by the app on that device.

## Android backup

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, Android may include Emogy's app data in the backup
of your device that is stored in **your own Google Account**, so it can be restored on a new device.
That backup is performed by Android and Google under Google's terms and privacy policy. We cannot
access, read or delete those backup copies. You can turn backup off or delete existing backups in
your device's Android settings or your Google Account settings.

## Permissions

Emogy requests exactly one Android permission:

* `VIBRATE` – to vibrate briefly on taps, matches and mistakes when haptic feedback is enabled. It
  is a normal permission and shows no prompt.

The app does **not** request internet, location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos, files,
notifications or any other permission.

## Ads, purchases and third parties

* **No advertising.** There are no ad SDKs and no ads of any kind.
* **No in-app purchases.** Coins are earned by playing, cannot be bought with real money and have no
  monetary value.
* **No third-party services.** The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution, social
  or cloud SDKs, and it shares no data with anyone.

## Children's privacy

Emogy is intended for a **general audience aged 13 and over** and is not directed at children
under 13. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children, and contains no
ads, purchases, chat or external links. If you believe a child has sent personal information to us,
please contact us using the address below; however, the app itself has no way to send us data.

## Security

Because Emogy does not transmit data, there is no data in transit to intercept and no server to
breach. Locally stored game data is protected by Android's app sandbox and, if you enable it, by your
device's own lock screen and encryption.
