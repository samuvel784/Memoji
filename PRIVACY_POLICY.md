# Privacy Policy – Memoji: The Memory Game

_Last updated: 25 October 2026_

Memoji ("the app") is an offline puzzle game.

**Data we collect:** none. The app has no account system and does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal information.

**Data stored on your device:** your game progress (unlocked level, stars, best times), settings (sound, vibration, theme, Zen mode) and simple statistics (games played, daily streak). This data is stored locally and, if you have Android Backup enabled, may be backed up to your own Google account by Android so it can be restored on a new device. We cannot access it.

**Permissions:** the app only uses the VIBRATE permission to give haptic feedback. It does not access your location, camera, microphone, contacts, photos or files, and it has no internet permission.

**Third parties:** the app contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting or social SDKs.

**Children:** the app contains no ads, purchases or links, and collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes:** if this policy changes, the updated version will be published with the app update.
